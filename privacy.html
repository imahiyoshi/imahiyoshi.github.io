Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

drive-backup is a personal-use application operated by its owner. It connects to Google Drive through the Google Drive API solely to copy the owner's own files from one of the owner's Google accounts to another of the owner's Google accounts, as a backup against account loss.

Data accessed

The application accesses the files and metadata in the owner's Google Drive only to the extent needed to perform the backup copy. No other person's data is accessed.

Data use and sharing

Google user data obtained through the API is used only for the backup described above. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train machine learning models.

Data storage and retention

Copied files are stored only in the owner's own Google Drive accounts. OAuth access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and can be revoked by the owner at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Contact

The owner can be reached at the email address listed as the user support email on the application's OAuth consent screen.
